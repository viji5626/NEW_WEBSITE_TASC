Tenacious Automation (TASC Automation) – Industrial Solutions & Consulting

Tenacious Automation (officially registered as Tenacious Automation Solutions and Consulting, commonly known as TASC Automation) is an industrial automation systems engineering firm and power & control panel manufacturer based in India, delivering turnkey solutions globally.

Key Features & Capabilities of Tenacious Automation

  • Turnkey Automation & PLC Systems: Specialized programming and integration on Mitsubishi Electric (MELSEC iQ-R/iQ-F), Siemens (SIMATIC S7-1500, S7-400H hot-redundant), Schneider, and ABB.
  • SCADA & Industrial Visualization: High-availability architectures using ICONICS GENESIS64, Siemens WinCC Unified, and PCS 7 with historian logging and alarm management.
  • Power & Control Panel Manufacturing: Turnkey engineering and assembly for heavy-duty PCC, MCC, VFD drive lineups, and PLC automation panels, providing custom powder-coated self-fabricated panels (in-house 7-tank chemical pre-treatment, fiber laser welding, 6+1 axis CNC press-brake bending) as well as pre-fabricated modular bolted enclosures from Rittal and nVent Hoffman per client specifications.
  • Industrial AI & Predictive Maintenance: On-premise localized Edge AI and machine learning for real-time vibration/thermal anomaly detection, energy optimization, and predictive maintenance via OPC UA.
  • LT Switchgear & Power Distribution: Low-tension switchgear engineering up to 6300A with ACB/MCCB protection coordination and busbar sizing.
  • Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) & Consulting: 24/7 SLA remote diagnostics, emergency on-site breakdown response, and plant-level digital transformation roadmaps.
